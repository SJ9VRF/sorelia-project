# Résumé wording — SORELIA

## Project line
**SORELIA — Moving Failure-Frontier Optimization for Interactive Agents** · Aura Yavary

## Strong safe bullet
Designed and built an end-to-end agent-reliability research system that tracks failure mechanisms across policy updates, measures risk extinction/emergence and displacement, reallocates equal verified-data budgets, and evaluates closed-loop interventions with repeated trials, leakage gates, negative controls, state-based grading, and a real Chromium/Playwright backend.

## Research-engineering bullet
Implemented provider-agnostic interactive-agent interfaces, longitudinal failure matching with ambiguity/split/merge diagnostics, counterfactual task verification, reproducible benchmark manifests, truthful token/cost accounting, and claim-to-evidence release audits; retained adverse/null runs rather than tuning the story around positive outcomes.

## Public-evidence rule
Do **not** write that SORELIA beats SOTA/frontier agents until real post-training studies satisfy the paper-scale gate. Do not use synthetic smoke percentages as résumé impact metrics.

## Future result template
Demonstrated that transition-aware failure-frontier curricula improved [metric] by [measured effect] versus [strong baseline] under equal verified-data/optimization budgets across [models, environments, seeds], while reducing [persistent/expanding/emergent risk] on held-out environments.
