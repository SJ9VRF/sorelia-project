# SORELIA demo narration

## 75-second public demo
**0–10s** — “This is a retained Chromium trajectory, not a scripted success video. The task eventually succeeds, but the interesting part is the failures inside the trajectory.”

**10–25s** — Show `browser-real-0001`. “The agent takes a correct action, then makes a memory failure. SORELIA records the pre-state, action, expected state, observed state, failure type, severity, recoverability, and the local correction.”

**25–40s** — “A second memory failure occurs later and is locally recovered. A final success bit alone would hide both events.”

**40–58s** — Switch to the moving-frontier figure. “Across policy updates, SORELIA links mechanisms and asks whether they became extinct, contracted, persisted, expanded, or emerged. In this engineering smoke, success stayed at 90%, but introduced risk exceeded removed risk by 1.33×.”

**58–70s** — “That transition becomes a curriculum signal under a fixed verified-data budget. Static priority, random, difficulty, and other baselines receive the same budget.”

**70–75s** — “The current release proves the measurement and experimental system. Frontier-model superiority remains an experiment, not a claim.”
