# EXP-005 — Repeated-trial / multiseed inference

**Evidence tier:** engineering smoke / methodology validation, not frontier-model performance evidence.

## Hypothesis
Are apparent differences stable under repeated stochastic evaluation?

## Setup
Repeated task trials across multiple seeds with paired bootstrap and paired randomization tests.

## Result
Small retained runs produced mixed effects and intervals that often included zero; no stable advantage was established.

## Interpretation
Single-draw agent evaluation was too brittle for claims.

## Next decision
Make repeated trials and paired inference mandatory in paper-scale protocol.
