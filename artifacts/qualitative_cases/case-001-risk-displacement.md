# Case 001 — Flat success, worse failure frontier

**Source:** `artifacts/v110_smoke/metrics.json`, iteration 2.

- Task success before update: 0.90
- Task success after update: 0.90
- Risk removed: 0.4485
- Risk introduced: 0.5949
- Risk displacement ratio: 1.3263
- Frontier transitions: 2 contracting, 2 extinct, 2 emergent

## Why this mattered
A scalar success metric would call the update neutral. The frontier view showed that newly introduced risk exceeded removed risk. This observation became the clearest motivation for SORELIA's longitudinal measurement objective.

## What changed next
The project elevated risk displacement from a diagnostic to a first-class reported metric and preserved the adverse update rather than tuning it away.
